# 🪓 BARBARIAN

### **THE FURY THAT WALKS**

**Role:** Melee Bruiser  
**Resource:** Rage  
**Weapon Fantasy:** Great Axe / Heavy Weapons  
**Core Mechanic:** Rage increases as the Barbarian fights. The closer he gets to defeat, the more dangerous he becomes.

```text
          ╱╲
         ╱  ╲       🪓
        ╱    ╲     /|
       ╱  🔥  ╲   / |
      ╱________╲    |
         ||||       / \
       THE RAGE
```

### 🔴 BERSERKER

**Fantasy:** Pure destruction.  
**Gameplay:** High burst damage, bleeding and Rage consumption.

**Signature abilities:**

- `Whirlwind` — Spin through enemies with escalating physical damage.
- `Blood Rush` — Charge forward and inflict Bleed.
- `Berserk` — Convert Rage into a temporary damage frenzy.
- `Rage Explosion` — Consume stored Rage to unleash an area blast.
- `Avatar of Rage` — Ultimate transformation that turns incoming damage into additional power.

**Visual identity:** Cracked armor, exposed muscles, red Rage particles and a weapon that glows brighter as Rage rises.

### 🟠 WARLORD

**Fantasy:** The battlefield commander.  
**Gameplay:** Area control, buffs and aggressive team support.

**Signature abilities:**

- `Cleaving Strike`
- `War Cry`
- `Commanding Roar`
- `Battle Standard`
- `Unstoppable Assault`

**Visual identity:** Heavy war banner, decorated armor, golden-red aura and commanding attack animations.

### ⚫ JUGGERNAUT

**Fantasy:** An unstoppable wall of steel.  
**Gameplay:** Tanking, Stuns and damage reduction.

**Signature abilities:**

- `Ground Slam`
- `Iron Skin`
- `Taunt`
- `Seismic Charge`
- `Colossus Form`

**Visual identity:** Massive armor, stone-like plating and heavy impact effects.

# 🔥 MAGE

### **THE ONE WHO SPEAKS TO THE ELEMENTS**

**Role:** Elemental Caster  
**Resource:** Mana  
**Weapon Fantasy:** Staff / Tome  
**Core Mechanic:** Spells build Arcane Power and unlock stronger elemental combinations.

```text

             🔥  ⚡  ❄️
```

### 🔥 PYROMANCER

**Fantasy:** Fire is not a spell. It is a hunger.

**Gameplay:** Burn, explosions and massive area damage.

**Signature abilities:**

- `Fireball`
- `Flame Wave`
- `Burning Ground`
- `Meteor`
- `Inferno`

**Visual identity:** Ember particles, burning robes and a constantly flickering flame core.

### ❄️ FROSTCALLER

**Fantasy:** The temperature drops before the enemy realizes the fight has begun.

**Gameplay:** Freeze, Slow and battlefield control.

**Signature abilities:**

- `Frost Bolt`
- `Ice Lance`
- `Blizzard`
- `Frozen Prison`
- `Absolute Zero`

**Visual identity:** Pale-blue magic, crystalline armor and frozen footprints.

### ⚡ ARCANIST

**Fantasy:** The Arcanist does not control magic. The Arcanist rewrites it.

**Gameplay:** Lightning, Mana manipulation and Arcane burst damage.

**Signature abilities:**

- `Chain Lightning`
- `Arcane Beam`
- `Mana Surge`
- `Arcane Nova`
- `Astral Cataclysm`

**Visual identity:** Floating runes, violet lightning and unstable arcane energy.

# 🗡️ ROGUE

### **THE SHADOW YOU NEVER SAW**

**Role:** Agile Assassin  
**Resource:** Energy  
**Weapon Fantasy:** Daggers / Bow  
**Core Mechanic:** Combo Points reward consecutive attacks and unlock powerful finishers.

```text

           /  |  \
```

### ☠️ ASSASSIN

**Fantasy:** One target. One opening. One dead enemy.

**Gameplay:** Critical hits, Poison, Stealth and Execute mechanics.

**Signature abilities:**

- `Poison Strike`
- `Dash`
- `Shadow Step`
- `Execute`
- `Death Mark`

**Visual identity:** Black leather, poisoned blades and a crimson critical-hit flash.

### 🌑 SHADOWDANCER

**Fantasy:** A living silhouette that refuses to stay in one place.

**Gameplay:** Teleports, Evasion, Clones and extreme mobility.

**Signature abilities:**

- `Shadow Blade`
- `Blink`
- `Smoke Bomb`
- `Shadow Clone`
- `Dance of Shadows`

**Visual identity:** Multiple silhouettes, smoke trails and fragmented shadow effects.

### 🏹 RANGER

**Fantasy:** The Hollow is dangerous. The Ranger is more dangerous from a distance.

**Gameplay:** Ranged damage, Poison and Traps.

**Signature abilities:**

- `Multishot`
- `Poison Arrow`
- `Trap`
- `Piercing Shot`
- `Rain of Arrows`

**Visual identity:** Hooded hunter, enchanted bow and green poison effects.

# 🎨 VISUAL LANGUAGE

RabbitHollow's Champion presentation is designed around a **dark pixel-fantasy contrast system**:

| Champion | Primary Motif | Combat Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| 🪓 Barbarian | Fire / Blood | Impact |
| 🔥 Mage | Elements / Arcane | Destruction |
| 🗡️ Rogue | Shadow / Poison | Precision |
| 🛡️ Paladin | Light / Gold | Protection |

The goal is that a player can recognize a Champion's combat identity **before reading a single ability description**.

# 🧬 SUBCLASS EVOLUTION

Every Subclass is intended to evolve through three stages:

```text
LEVEL 1
  │
  ├── Identity Passive
  │
  ▼
LEVEL 10
  │
  ├── Core Skill Unlocks
  │
  ▼
LEVEL 25
  │
  ├── Build-Defining Choice
  │
  ▼
LEVEL 50
  │
  ├── Advanced Skill Modification
  │
  ▼
LEVEL 75
  │
  ├── Signature Mechanic
  │
  ▼
LEVEL 100
  │
  └── Ultimate Mastery
```

The same Subclass should support different builds. A Berserker focused on Bleed should not necessarily play the same way as a Berserker focused on Rage explosions.

# ✨ THE HOLLOW'S DESIGN RULE

> **A Hero is not defined by the weapon they carry.**
>
> **A Hero is defined by what happens when they enter the darkness.**

The visual concepts in this document are intended as original art direction for the RabbitHollow project. They can later be replaced or extended with final pixel-art sprites, portraits, animations and in-game character sheets without changing the underlying gameplay data.
