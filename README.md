# 🌑 THE HOLLOW IS WAITING

<div align="center">
<img src="https://placehold.co/1200x550/161616/FFFFFF?text=THE+HOLLOW" alt="The Hollow">
</div>

RabbitHollow is a dark pixel-art Action RPG where every journey begins in the small town of **Hollowmere**.

Beneath the world lies an ancient network of forgotten ruins, corrupted forests, abandoned mines and impossible dungeons.

Nobody knows what created the Hollow.

Nobody knows how deep it goes.

And nobody who has reached its deepest chambers has ever returned.

**You are about to find out.**

A brutal melee Champion who turns rage into power.

**Subclasses:** Berserker · Warlord · Juggernaut

## 🔥 MAGE

A master of elemental destruction.

**Subclasses:** Pyromancer · Frostcaller · Arcanist

**Signature Skills:** `Fireball` · `Blizzard` · `Chain Lightning` · `Meteor`

## 🗡️ ROGUE

A fast and deadly Champion who strikes from the shadows.

**Subclasses:** Assassin · Shadowdancer · Ranger

**Signature Skills:** `Dash` · `Poison Strike` · `Shadow Step` · `Multishot`

## 🛡️ PALADIN

A holy warrior capable of protecting allies and destroying enemies.

**Subclasses:** Crusader · Templar · Guardian

**Signature Skills:** `Shield Bash` · `Holy Strike` · `Divine Shield` · `Light's Blessing`

# 🎮 REAL-TIME COMBAT

<div align="center">
<img src="https://placehold.co/1200x600/151515/FFFFFF?text=REAL--TIME+COMBAT" alt="RabbitHollow Combat">
</div>

RabbitHollow uses fast real-time Action RPG combat.

```text
LEFT CLICK     Basic Attack
RIGHT CLICK    Primary Skill
1 - 4          Active Skills
Q              Special Ability
E              Secondary Ability
SPACE          Dodge
```

Combat includes Basic Attacks, Elemental Damage, Critical Hits, Damage Over Time, Freeze, Stun, Poison, Shields, Healing, Dodge and Buffs & Debuffs.

# 👹 MONSTERS & 💀 ELITES

<div align="center">
<img src="https://placehold.co/1200x500/151515/FFFFFF?text=MONSTER+ENCOUNTERS" alt="Monsters">

Elite modifiers include:

🔥 Molten · ❄️ Frozen · ⚡ Electrified · 🩸 Vampiric · ☠️ Poisonous · 💨 Fast · ♻️ Regenerating · 🛡️ Shielded · 💥 Explosive

**Vampiric + Frozen + Bone Knight**

Good luck.

# 🔐 GOOGLE ACCOUNT

---

# 💾 OFFLINE-FIRST

RabbitHollow is designed to work offline.

Local progression can be stored using **IndexedDB**.

The game can save Champions, Levels, Skills, Items, Inventory, Equipment, Quests and Dungeon Progress.

Internet connection is optional for the core experience.
